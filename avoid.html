Things to avoid

  • PCB with headers (signal integrity issues). Different power domains: power pins (a voltage higher than logic power) next to data pins. A good example: Arduino UNO R3 has separate headers for power and data.
  • GPIOs, not knowing which pin does what. If repairs are needed, usually schematics are not available and thus repair is hard. TBD: link to a consultancy firm.
  • Arduino IDE, due to inaccessibility of libraries. Better: vs code
  • TVs that: don't have a detachable power cord; are bottom edge lit; timing board is merged into main or source board; power source is internal; smart TVs (is an equipment that has multiple functionalities in one package).
  • XLR (mic connector) on a battery related product.
  • DC-DC IC with multiple outputs.
  • Raspberry pi. Advantage: power efficiency. Disadvantage: not repairable.
  • GNU related SW, GPL license. Good: BSL, Apache, MIT
  • Ads, commercials: a form of forced waste of time.
  • Products of huge global companies, e.g. S&P500, because of profit prioritization.
  • SpearItAnimal - Catamaran Lagoon 450s fiberglass repairs and interior remodeling, alcohol promotion and inappropriate jokes on a public platform.
