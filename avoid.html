Things to !AVOID!:

PCB with classic headers for data signals (signal integrity issues). Example: apple solders memory onto main board.

PCB with connectors having data signals near to power pins (with a voltage outside of logic level range). A good example: Arduino UNO R3 headers power and data are separated.

GPIOs, because now we don't know which pin does what. If repairs are needed, usually schematics are not available and thus repair is hard.

Arduino IDE, due to inaccessibility of libraries. Better: VS Code IntelliSense can open included headers in an instant

TVs that don't have a detachable power cord. (mostly Chinese). Good: Samsung, Philips, LGs...

TVs that are bottom edge lit, including some Samsung models. Due to gravity, prism is pressing on LEDs and they're getting hot.

TVs that main board contains timing board or power supply (Chinese models like TLC, Hisense)

TVs that timing board is merged into source board.

TVs having power supply inside.

TVs with a thin or glued display panel or glued backlight led strips. Hisense measures glue quantity by AI. If you use AI on a bad practice, it doesn't make it better.

Do not keep appliances powered 24/7 because its power supply standby lanes will have issues over time.

Some smart TVs do updates while in standby; avoid smart TVs.

Equipment having multiple functionalities in one package; avoid smart TVs.

XLR (mic connector) used as power connector on a battery related product.

DC-DC IC with multiple outputs. When it brakes, it's hard to fix back.

I2C, I3C or other address based protocols. If an IC bakes, an replacement after 10+ yrs will have another address.

Raspberry pi. Advantage: power efficiency. Disadvantage: definitely not repairable.

Mass-produced EVs, due to bad and harmful industries practices focused sole on profit margin.

Petrol based cars. Love BEVs, but not in the way they are actually made.

Tesla EV due to door handles issues, motor coolant leaks, glued batteries, different port for diagnostics; a reliable car would be nice, not a stunt car.

GNU related SW, GPL license. Good: BSL, Apache, MIT

Ads, commercials: a form of forced waste of time.

Products of huge global companies, e.g. S&P500, because of profit prioritization.
