Things TO !AVOID!:

PCB with classic headers for data signals (signal integrity issues).

PCB with connectors having data signals near to power pins (with a voltage outside of logic level high/low range). A good example: Arduino UNO R3 headers power and data are separated.

GPIOs, because now we don't know which pin does what. If repairs are needed, usually schematics are not available and thus repair is hard.

Arduino IDE, due to inaccessibility of libraries. Better: VS Code

TVs that don't have a detachable power cord.

TVs that are bottom edge lit, including some Samsung models

TVs that main board contains timing board or power supply (Chinese models like TLC, Hisense)

TVs that timing board is merged into source board

TVs having an internal power source

Equipment having multiple functionalities in one package, e.g. smart TVs.

XLR (mic connector) on a battery related product.

DC-DC IC with multiple outputs.

Raspberry pi. Advantage: power efficiency. Disadvantage: not repairable.

GNU related SW, GPL license. Good: BSL, Apache, MIT

Ads, commercials: a form of forced waste of time.

Products of huge global companies, e.g. S&P500, because of profit prioritization.
